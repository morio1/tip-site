THE CONSTITUTION OF THE INCUBATOR PLATFORM
PREAMBLE
We, the youth and community members of Langata Sub-County, recognizing the urgent need to address climate change and its impacts on our communities, and committed to empowering young people as agents of environmental change, do hereby establish this Community-Based Organization (CBO) to promote climate resilience, environmental sustainability, and youth development.
ARTICLE 1 - NAME AND LOCATION
1.1 Name
The name of the Community Based Organization shall be The Incubator Platform hereinafter referred to as TIP.

1.2 Location
The CBO shall be based in Langata Sub-County, Nairobi county, and shall operate within this jurisdiction and surrounding areas as necessary.
ARTICLE 2 - PURPOSE OF THE CONSTITUTION
The purpose of the constitution is to form a legal association between the members, the community, partners and other associated organizations.
The community-based organization being:
I. A non-profit organization.
II. Based on the spirit of community service in tandem with our mission and vision statements and in line with our objectives powered by our core values.
ARTICLE 3 - VISION, MISSION, GOALS, AND OBJECTIVES
3.1 Vision
To create a thriving, resilient community where youth are empowered, the environment is protected, economic opportunities are sustainable, and public health is accessible for all.

3.2 Mission
To drive holistic community development by integrating youth empowerment, environmental sustainability, economic growth, and public health initiatives, ensuring a healthier, more prosperous future for all generations.

3.3 Goals
a) Youth Empowerment & Development: Equip youth with skills, knowledge, and opportunities.
b) Environmental Sustainability: Promote eco-friendly practices and conservation.
c) Economic Sustainability: Encourage sustainable business practices and entrepreneurship.
d) Public Health & Well-being: Enhance awareness and access to healthcare.

3.4 Specific Objectives
i) Offer workshops and mentorship programs.
ii) Establish youth-led local projects.
iii) Implement environmental education programs.
iv) Promote sustainable agricultural practices.
v) Organize clean-up drives, tree planting, and eco-tourism.
vi) Provide training in green entrepreneurship.
vii) Foster partnerships for sustainable economic development.
viii) Conduct health awareness campaigns.
ix) Partner with healthcare providers for accessible services.
ARTICLE 4 - AREA OF OPERATION
TIP shall operate within Langata Sub-County as its primary area, with exemptions for extended projects through partnerships and capacity-building activities.
ARTICLE 5 - CORE VALUES AND CODE OF CONDUCT
5.1 Core Values
Integrity, transparency, accountability, respect for diversity, and sustainability.

5.2 Code of Conduct
Members shall:
a) Act with integrity, transparency, and accountability.
b) Respect diversity and inclusion.
c) Avoid conflicts of interest.
d) Protect organizational resources.
e) Maintain confidentiality.
f) Promote environmental conservation.

Violations may result in disciplinary action including suspension or expulsion.
ARTICLE 6 - MEMBERSHIP
6.1 Categories
a) Ordinary Members.
b) Associate Members.
c) Honorary Members.

6.2 Eligibility
a) Open to all supportive persons.
b) Minimum age 18.
c) Residing in Kenya or approved abroad.
d) Under 18 not eligible.
e) Application in writing.
f) Board vets applications.

6.3 Fees
a) Registration fee: KES 1,000.
b) Paid via cash, M-Pesa, or bank.
c) Members pay determined contributions.

6.4 Cessation
Membership ceases due to:
a) Resignation.
b) Failure to pay fees.
c) Death/incapacitation.
d) Disciplinary expulsion.
ARTICLE 7 - ORGANIZATION STRUCTURE AND GOVERNANCE
7.1 Organs
I. General Meeting
II. Board
III. Sub-committees

7.2 Board Composition (7 members)
i. Chairperson
ii. Deputy Chairperson
iii. Secretary General
iv. Administrative Secretary
v. Treasurer
vi. Gender & Inclusion Officer
vii. Climate Action Coordinator

7.3 Meetings
Quarterly; quorum 4 members.

7.4 Board Powers
a) Vet members.
b) Make decisions on behalf of TIP.
c) Manage resources.
d) Lead official meetings and engagements.
e) Perform assigned responsibilities.

7.5 Discipline
A Board seat is vacated for:
I. Unsound mind.
II. Six months unexplained absence.
III. Removal by resolution.
IV. Removal from membership.
V. Slandering the organization.
VI. Gross misconduct or financial impropriety.
ARTICLE 8 - PRIVILEGES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
8.1 Rights
a) Access to trainings and workshops.
b) Priority for tasks and opportunities.
c) Networking and job links.
d) Dividends from income projects.
e) Celebrations policy for events.
f) Bereavement support.

8.2 Responsibilities
a) Uphold constitution and values.
b) Participate actively.
c) Participate in democratic processes.
ARTICLE 9 - DUTIES OF BOARD MEMBERS
Chairperson:
- Chairs meetings, signs documents, spokesperson.

Deputy Chairperson:
- Assists Chairperson, coordinates committees.

Secretary General:
- Operational head, partnerships, spokesperson, mobilization.

Administrative Secretary:
- Admin, records, notices, meeting minutes.

Treasurer:
- Financial reporting, managing funds, signatory.

Gender & Inclusion Officer:
- Promote equality and inclusion.

Climate Action Coordinator:
- Manage climate programs, partnerships, documentation.
ARTICLE 10 - GENERAL MEETINGS
10.1 AGM
- 14-day notice, held annually, quorum 1/3.

10.2 SGM/EGM
- 7-day notice.

10.3 Meetings Procedure
- Convened by Chairperson or 1/3 of members.
- Decisions require 2/3 quorum.
- One vote per member; Chairperson has tie-breaking vote.
ARTICLE 11 - FINANCES
11.1 Sources
a) Fees.
b) Grants and donations.
c) Income activities.
d) Other legal sources.

11.2 Management
a) Managed by Treasurer and Board.
b) Funds used only for the organization.
c) Bank accounts in TIP's name.
d) Two signatories required (Chairperson, Secretary General, Treasurer).
ARTICLE 12 - DISSOLUTION
12.1 Dissolution requires:
a) Two-thirds vote.
b) Quorum above 67%.

12.2 Assets
Remaining assets transferred to similar organizations; not distributed to members.
ARTICLE 13 - AMENDMENT
Requires written proposal, 28-day notice, and 2/3 majority vote.
ARTICLE 14 - GENERAL PROVISIONS
TIP is non-partisan, non-sectarian, non-discriminatory. Must comply with laws. Official languages: English/Kiswahili. Seal kept by Administrative Secretary.
ARTICLE 15 - ADOPTION
This constitution was adopted on 28th October 2025  at Nairobi.
