Preamble

"We, the youth and community members of Langata Sub-County, recognizing the urgent need to address climate change and its impacts on our communities, do hereby establish this organization to promote climate resilience, environmental sustainability, and youth development."

Art. 3.1 — Vision

A thriving, resilient community where youth are empowered and the environment is protected.

Economic opportunities are sustainable, and public health is accessible for all generations.

Art. 3.2 — Mission

Holistic community development through integration of youth, environment, economy, and health.

Ensuring a healthier, more prosperous future for all generations through sustained, community-led action.

Article 3.3

Goals

Four interlocking pillars that guide TIP's programs and investments.

A

Youth Empowerment

Equip youth with skills, knowledge, and opportunities through workshops, mentorship, and youth-led local projects.

B

Environmental Sustainability

Promote eco-friendly practices, conservation, clean-up drives, tree planting, and eco-tourism initiatives.

C

Economic Sustainability

Encourage sustainable business practices, green entrepreneurship, and partnerships for economic development.

D

Public Health

Enhance awareness and access to healthcare through awareness campaigns and provider partnerships.

Articles of
the Constitution

Select any article to expand its provisions. All articles were ratified at the founding general meeting on 28 October 2025.

1.1 Name

The name of the Community Based Organization shall be The Incubator Platform, hereinafter referred to as TIP.

1.2 Location

The CBO shall be based in Langata Sub-County, Nairobi County, and shall operate within this jurisdiction and surrounding areas as necessary.

The purpose of the constitution is to form a legal association between the members, the community, partners and other associated organizations. TIP is a non-profit organization, grounded in the spirit of community service in tandem with our mission and vision statements, and in line with our objectives, powered by our core values.

TIP shall operate within Langata Sub-County as its primary area, with exemptions for extended projects through partnerships and capacity-building activities.

5.1 Core Values

Integrity, transparency, accountability, respect for diversity, and sustainability.

5.2 Code of Conduct

Members shall act with integrity, transparency, and accountability; respect diversity and inclusion; avoid conflicts of interest; protect organizational resources; maintain confidentiality; and promote environmental conservation. Violations may result in disciplinary action including suspension or expulsion.

6.1 Categories

Ordinary Members · Associate Members · Honorary Members

6.2 Eligibility

Open to all supportive persons aged 18 and above, residing in Kenya or approved abroad. Applications are made in writing and vetted by the Board.

6.3 Fees

Registration fee: KES 1,000. Payable via cash, M-Pesa, or bank. Members pay contributions as determined by the Board.

6.4 Cessation

Membership ceases due to resignation, failure to pay fees, death or incapacitation, or disciplinary expulsion.

7.1 Organs

General Meeting · Board · Sub-committees

7.2 Board Composition

Seven members: Chairperson, Deputy Chairperson, Secretary General, Administrative Secretary, Treasurer, Gender & Inclusion Officer, and Climate Action Coordinator.

7.3 Meetings

Quarterly, with a quorum of 4 members.

7.4 Board Powers

Vet members · Make decisions on behalf of TIP · Manage resources · Lead official meetings and engagements · Perform assigned responsibilities.

8.1 Member Rights

Access to trainings and workshops · Priority for tasks and opportunities · Networking and job links · Dividends from income projects · Celebrations policy for events · Bereavement support.

8.2 Responsibilities

Uphold the constitution and values · Participate actively · Participate in democratic processes.

10.1 AGM

14-day notice, held annually. Quorum: one-third of members.

10.2 SGM / EGM

7-day notice. Convened by the Chairperson or one-third of members.

10.3 Procedure

Decisions require a two-thirds quorum. One vote per member; Chairperson holds the tie-breaking vote.

11.1 Sources

Membership fees · Grants and donations · Income-generating activities · Other legal sources.

11.2 Management

Managed by the Treasurer and Board. Funds used only for organizational purposes. Bank accounts held in TIP's name. Two signatories required from: Chairperson, Secretary General, Treasurer.

12.1 Procedure

Dissolution requires a two-thirds vote with a quorum above 67%.

12.2 Assets

Remaining assets are transferred to similar organizations and are not distributed to members.

Amendments require a written proposal, 28-day notice to all members, and a two-thirds majority vote at a general meeting.

TIP is non-partisan, non-sectarian, and non-discriminatory. The organization must comply with all applicable laws. Official languages: English and Kiswahili. The official seal is kept by the Administrative Secretary.

Article 15 — Adoption

Adopted in Nairobi
28 October 2025

This constitution was duly adopted at the founding meeting of The Incubator Platform by the elected Board and founding members.
